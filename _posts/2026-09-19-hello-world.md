This is a sample post. Delete it once you have something real to say.

Posts live in `_posts/` and are named `YYYY-MM-DD-slug.md`. Each one needs a
front matter block at the top with at least a `title` and a `date`; the optional
`description` shows up as the one-line blurb under the title on the home page.

## Images

Drop images in `assets/images/` and reference them like this:

```markdown
![Alt text](/assets/images/some-picture.jpg)
```
